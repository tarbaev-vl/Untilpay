Home Budget

Privacy Policy

Home Budget is a personal, non-commercial, self-hosted application. It is run by its owner on their own server and is used only by the owner to keep track of their own finances. It is not offered to the public.

What data is processed

Expenses, income and recurring payments entered by the owner, transactions imported from CSV bank statements, and, if bank sync is enabled, transactions of the owner’s own bank accounts received through Enable Banking after the owner authorises access at the bank.

For bank sync the application stores the Enable Banking session identifier, the consent expiry date, account names and the last four characters of the account number. Bank credentials are entered only on the bank’s own pages and never pass through this application.

Where the data is kept

All data is stored in a database on the owner’s own server. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising or analytics.

Third parties

Enable Banking Oy provides access to bank account information under the owner’s consent. If the optional budget advice feature is switched on, aggregated spending figures are sent to the AI provider chosen by the owner; with a local model nothing leaves the server.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. Access to a bank can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the bank in the application or at the bank itself.

Contact

The application has no users other than its owner, who is also the data controller.
