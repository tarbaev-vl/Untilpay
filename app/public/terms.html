Terms of Use

Home Budget is a personal, non-commercial, self-hosted application operated by its owner for their own use. It is not a public service and no accounts are offered to other people.

Use

The application records expenses and income and, if bank sync is enabled, reads transactions of the owner’s own bank accounts through Enable Banking. It only reads account information; it cannot initiate payments.

Bank access

Access to bank accounts is granted by the owner at the bank, is limited in time and can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the bank in the application or at the bank itself.

No warranty

The application is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Figures shown, including any budget advice, are for the owner’s information only and are not financial advice.

Changes

The owner may change or shut down the application at any time.
